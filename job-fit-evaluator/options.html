On-page button and shortcut

Shared by every profile.

Keyboard shortcut

Evaluates the posting in the current tab, on any site.

On-page button

A card in the corner of job postings that shows the saved score and evaluates with one click. It's off everywhere until you turn it on for a site, and each site is a separate Chrome permission you can take back.

Job boards

A board is turned on everywhere at once — every country, every employer — with one Chrome permission.

Other sites

Company career sites (Jibe, Eightfold, a Greenhouse board embedded on the company's own site) are turned on one at a time: open one, then tick “Show the Tino button” in the Tino popup.

Data

Every profile

A backup holds profiles, model settings (never your OpenAI API key) and every tracked job — everything the CSV leaves out. Uninstalling the extension erases all of it.

Extraction coverage

How often job pages carried structured data that could have filled in something the extractor missed. Useful when reporting a site that reads badly.
